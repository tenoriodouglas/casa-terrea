# casa-terrea

Preliminary design of a single-story house on a 12 × 41 m lot, viewable in any browser (desktop and mobile).

**Live site:** https://tenoriodouglas.github.io/casa-terrea/

## Site
- **Planta**: dimensioned 2D floor plan (drawn as seen from the street), area table, green-area and accessibility notes.
- **Maquete 3D**: orbit model with preset views and image capture.
- **Passeio 3D**: real-time first-person walkthrough (WASD + mouse on desktop, joystick + drag on mobile).

Deep links: `#planta`, `#maquete`, `#passeio`.

## Program
- Genkan entrance (stone floor, shoe cabinet with mirror, bench; step-free).
- Closed living room with a single double sliding door to the kitchen hall; dining room integrated with a compact kitchen (oven tower, island) and a pantry.
- Powder room and winter garden between the living area and the bedroom wing.
- 3 bedrooms (1 master suite) + home office. The bedroom corridor (1.30 m) gives access to the laundry (side door to the clothesline), shared bathroom, bedrooms 2 and 3, master suite and office.
- Master suite: bedroom, 10 m² accessible bathroom and 10 m² walk-in closet with island.
- Compact covered grill: sink, wood-fired grill and pizza oven sharing one chimney, herb planter beside it.
- Lawn, fruit trees, compost bin and 5 raised vegetable beds (45 m²).
- Accessibility (NBR 9050 basis): 0.90 m doors, 1.50 m turning circles in bathrooms, curbless showers.
- Wood floors throughout (wood-look porcelain in wet areas); lawn outside, including a grass driveway. Ceiling height 3.00 m.

`index.html` is the whole site (Three.js r128 from CDN).
